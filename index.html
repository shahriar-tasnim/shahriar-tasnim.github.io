About me

Engineering across the full system

My work combines robotics software, RGB-D perception, electromechanical integration, embedded hardware, and industrial troubleshooting. I earned my Master of Engineering Science in Electrical & Computer Engineering from Lamar University-USA, where my thesis focused on autonomous robotic quality sorting using 6DoF pose estimation and depth-based anomaly detection.

I enjoy taking systems from concept through integration, validation, commissioning, and root-cause analysis. I am especially interested in robotics deployment, field service, automation and controls, and test or validation engineering.
